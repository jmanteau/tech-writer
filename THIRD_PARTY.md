## Signs of AI writing

The AI tells H1 to H24 paraphrase ideas from [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing). That page is advice from WikiProject AI Cleanup, published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
